About

Glitchcore Universe is a faceless AI-generated short-form comedy series exploring digital personas, recursive simulations, and the texture of online existence. Episodes are produced as 10-second vertical videos for short-form platforms.

The series follows three characters — NPC_Dave, GlitchedOut, and Feral Algorithm — across seasons that build a shared universe. Every episode is generated with AI tools and disclosed as AI-generated content per platform policies.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Glitchcore Universe (the "Project", "we", or "us") handles information in connection with this website (the "Site") and our published video content. We have designed our operations to minimize the personal data we touch.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

This Site does not:

  • Use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies.
  • Run third-party analytics scripts (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no Meta scripts).

  • Sell, share, or disclose any data, because we do not collect any.

This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub Pages may log standard HTTP request information (such as IP address, user agent, referring URL, and timestamps) for operational purposes such as security and abuse prevention. This logging is performed by GitHub and is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not access, retain, or analyze these logs ourselves.

Glitchcore Universe content is distributed on third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. When you view, like, comment on, or otherwise interact with our content on those platforms, those interactions are governed by each platform's own privacy policy:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy
  • YouTube / Google Privacy Policy

We do not have access to identifying information about viewers on those platforms beyond aggregate, anonymized engagement metrics that the platforms display to creators (e.g., total view counts, total like counts).

5. AI-Generated Content

All Glitchcore Universe video content is generated using AI tools. The content depicts fictional characters and does not represent real people. The AI generation process does not involve collecting, training on, or processing any data about viewers of the Site or the content.

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Distribution platforms have their own age-restriction policies that govern who may view our content there.
